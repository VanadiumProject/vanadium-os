## JH7110
- Clock speed: 1.5GHz
- Quad-core
- LPDDR4/DDR4/LPDDR3/DDR3 [external RAM](../02_Architecture/Memory_Map.md)(DDR3 will be the one to be used to evade unnecessary complexity)
- GPU supports: OpenCL 3.0, OpenGL ES 3.2 and Vulkan 1.2
- Support 2x PCIe 2.0, 1 lane(Good for [expansion Connector](../03_Hardware/Expansion_Connector.md) idea)
- eMMC 5.0
- 5 W max consumption(only under non-ideal conditions), 0.13 W power leakage.
