## Real Connection
The current idea it's to utilize the [CPU](../02_Architecture/Main_CPU.md) support for PCIe 2.0 and use a M.2 Key E connector to connect the expansion device.
## Official Support
For now, we believe that the second screen expansion, it's our flagship one, and we'd offer official support out of the box on our [SDK](../05_SDK_HAL_/SDK_Overview.md). Although, our idea it's to make it possible for anyone to create their own.
## Homebrew' Support
We want the user to be able to develop their own firmware to run on their device and the OS to identify it and let them use it, per example: An expansion of a dedicated keyboard, or if you are crazy enough(and find a reason for) an eGPU.
