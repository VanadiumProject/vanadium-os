## Why RISC-V?
- Low power consumption
- Open Source philosophy at its core
- Simplicity(RISC design)
- Modern choice
There are some motives to not choose it as well:
- The modern choice is a double-edged sword, at the same time it's a risk(no pun intended) that I need to take, it's faith on a new architecture
## Why the JH7110 and not others?
>"This is something that truly matters and it's a good thing to discuss about, there are some whys to not choose it, as it goes against part of my vision of transparency, but those drawbacks are things that I can't escape, targeting what I am, 3D at two screens in 720p resolution, the GPU development situation is of black boxes, and there isn't a real and plausible way to escape it. As for reasons to choose it instead of any one else, it supports what I want the console to have and leaves room for improvement and comfort for the system, per example: The Clock speed is more than enough for the dual screen at 60Hz, and the OS to run without any hiccups; The support for GPU interfaces means, so that people that are already in the development scene doesn't have a massive barrier to entering it coming from other hardware targets."
