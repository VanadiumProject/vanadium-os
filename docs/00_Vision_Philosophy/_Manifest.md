#### **Motivation:**
> The project started from a frustration I've had, not only growing up, but as a proper consumer now.
> Game consoles are black boxes by design, they are intentionally opaque systems, with no official way of truly understanding them unless you were involved in making them.
> My objective as an architect is to change that. To allow the end user to truly understand what they have in hand.
#### **Beliefs:**
- We believe in user sovereignty:
	- Every user should be able to understand, modify, and repair the hardware and software they own. Ownership is real, not just a label.
- We believe in elegant simplicity: 
	- Complexity should exist only where it truly serves a purpose. Every design choice should serve clarity and usability.
- We believe freedom without tolls:
	- Users should never face artificial barriers or costs to explore, modify, or create with their devices.
#### **Commitments:**
- Open source: All hardware designs, software, and documentation will be publicly available and maintainable.
- Repairability: Every console is designed to be repairable, with modular components and detailed guides.
- Transparency: Decisions about design, architecture, and updates will be documented for the community.
- Respect for the users: Users control their own devices; no forced obsolescence, artificial lock-ins, or bricking consoles.
#### **Anti-Goals**
- Not aiming to be a mass-market console.
- Not prioritizing profit over user experience.
- Not creating an ecosystem where users are locked in
- Not designed for trends; the project values long-term relevance over hype.
#### **Call to Action**
- This project is for those who want to explore, understand, and create.
- Join us, experiment, and help shape a platform built for understanding.
- The Vanadium is a platform, a tool, and a challenge. **Welcome to the journey!**
### **Closing**
> "Admiration comes from understanding."
> "Complexity is a engineer's problem, the end user shouldn't be the target of not even a bit of that, our true objective is to make the user's experience truly excellent and have nothing that would make any user do anything but play and enjoy it"
